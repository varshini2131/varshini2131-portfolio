01 / Featured · End-to-end BI

AdventureWorks — Four-Tool Sales Analytics

An end-to-end sales analytics project using AdventureWorks data, moving from raw tables to a modeled dataset, business queries, and dashboards across four tools.

  • SQL: Combined fact sales tables with UNION ALL, used LEFT JOIN to enrich records, and queried product, customer, and sales details.
  • Excel: Practiced data cleaning and transformation with Power Query, formulas, PivotTables, and charts for sales, profit, orders, and time-based trends.
  • Power BI: Modeled sales data, created measures for sales amount, production cost, and profit, and designed KPI-led interactive reports.
  • Tableau: Built comparative views for sales, cost, and profit by date, product, customer, and region.
